# Staff & Management Guide

This guide is designed for Department Heads, Lab Heads, and IT Technicians who manage resources and personnel within their specific units.

## Managing Your Department

As a Department or Lab Head, you are responsible for the assets assigned to your area:

1.  **Inventory Monitoring**: Use the **Dashboard** to get an instant overview of your department's hardware health and assignment status.
2.  **Location Management**: Ensure all your Laboratories and Offices are correctly listed so hardware can be tracked accurately.
3.  **Staff Accounts**: You can create and manage accounts for your assistants and technicians. Make sure everyone has the correct role for their duties.

## Operational Tasks (Technicians & Assistants)

If your role involves direct hardware management:

*   **Adding Components**: Use the **New Component** button on the Components page to log new hardware. Remember to include the **Serial Number** for unique tracking!
*   **Status Updates**: Keeping the status (Good, Broken, etc.) up to date is critical for accurate reporting.
*   **Assignment**: Learn how to assign "Rogue" (unassigned) components to specific **Computer Sets** in your labs.

> **NOTE:** You only see resources and staff that belong to your assigned department. For global access, contact a System Administrator.
