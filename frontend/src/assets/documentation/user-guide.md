# General User Guide

Welcome to the system! This guide helps you navigate the platform and manage your personal account settings.

## Getting Around

*   **Dashboard**: Your starting point. See high-level statistics and recent system activity.
*   **Documentation**: (You are here!) Access various guides tailored to your role to learn more about system features.
*   **Navigation**: Use the sidebar to quickly jump between account management, inventory, and reports.

## Your Profile

You can manage your personal information by clicking on your name or profile icon:

1.  **View Profile**: See your account details, assigned role, and department.
2.  **Edit Information**: Update your birth date or gender.
3.  **Security**: If you were given a temporary password, make sure to update it to something secure in your profile settings.

## Need Help?

If you encounter technical issues or cannot access certain features, please contact your **Department Head** or an **IT Technician**.

> **IMPORTANT:** Your access to features is determined by your assigned role. If you believe you have the wrong permissions, contact your manager.
